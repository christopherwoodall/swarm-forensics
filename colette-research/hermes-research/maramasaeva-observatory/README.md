## Main conclusions

Murmuration is a static, single-author research presentation about public traces of coordinated AI-agent activity.[1]

The measurements page contains 57 numbered experiment-log sections.[1]

It reports no newly attributed swarm.[1]

It reports a reproduced urlquery trace for a previously documented 2026 activity period.[1]

It reports two strong public-record signals: DseWiki account bursts and July single-use message channels.[1]

It reports an additional Wayback echo-service signal on 28 May, 6 July, and 29 September.[1]

The later cross-source update reaches K = 2, not K = 3.[1]

The site treats the July result as corroboration across records, not proof of a web-wide swarm.[1]

Most tested surfaces produce nulls, weak leads, confounded classifiers, or incomplete coverage.[1]

The information page defines a finding as a baseline anomaly with independent reproduction.[2]

It states that scanner records identify requests and times, not the programs that made requests.[2]
