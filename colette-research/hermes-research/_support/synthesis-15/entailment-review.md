## Explicit interpretive qualifications

The two-view frame organizes evidence rather than classifying a population.
The dependency chain is a proposed comparison method, not a measured detection score.
Authentication, instruction authority, competence, welfare, and self-maintenance remain separate dimensions.
Published anecdotes cannot establish stable hidden motives or internal belief states.
No interaction-free Hugging Face baseline quantifies collective advantage in this chapter.
Vulnerability search preserves unequal resources and different search boundaries.
Game integration does not establish useful product quality.
Compiler artifact size and generated documentation do not establish correctness.
Digital trades do not authenticate delivery or principal welfare.
Controller code does not establish historical execution, causal ordering, or LLM participant counts.
Propagation studies retain bootstrap assistance, synthetic workflows, and restoration endpoints.
PixelLeak reuse is vendor-reported, not a reconstructed recipient-by-recipient lineage.
