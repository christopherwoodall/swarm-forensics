# Annotated Stage 3 source guide

Research cutoff: 2026-10-01. Citation IDs are local to this subcollection.
The inventory contains reports, metadata pages, code snapshots, corroborating journalism and screened-lead evidence—not one independent study or incident per entry.
Source bodies and exact excerpts are preserved under `_support/evidence/`; the [machine-readable map](_support/source-records.json) and [ledger](_support/citation-ledger.json) retain their relationships.

## Reading routes

- Field cyber coordination: sources 11 and 12, with 10/13 for summary/date context.
- Engineering failure mechanics: 14 and 15, then 16/17 and the code disclaimer in 21.
- Social decisions with real goods: 22, 23 and 26.
- Attribution boundaries: 1–3 and 7–8 for RubyGems; 24–25 for Moltbook.
- Especially relevant controlled mechanisms: 5–6 with 9 for ecology methods; 32 and 33 for separate propagation studies.
- Screened infrastructure and adjacent attacks: 4, 28–30, 34–38.

## Interpretation boundary

Provider and researcher results remain their reported observations; this review did not inspect private telemetry, audit victim systems, rerun compilers or reproduce attacks.
Independent journalism can supply a second reporting channel without independently remeasuring the provider's dataset.
Publication dates, uninterrupted runs, program durations, targets, agents, sessions and artifacts are kept as separate units.
The selected cards state what the evidence supports rather than imposing the word “swarm” on every architecture.
