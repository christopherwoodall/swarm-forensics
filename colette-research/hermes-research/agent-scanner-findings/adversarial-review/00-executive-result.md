# 00 — Executive result

The records support a narrower investigation than the brief describes.
They show archival activity, failed retrieval attempts, and repeated public-relay references.
They do not establish one operation or its cessation.

**SEC:** The current Wayback query returns 63 raw index entries and 62 URL keys, not 65.[1]
Thirty-nine entries occur during a 463-second interval.[1]
The main digest appears in 60 rows, including a revisit.[1]
The burst is real in this snapshot; agent initiation and Save Page Now use remain unproved.
The earliest archive entry predates the matching wiki activity.
The first bare-decimal capture comes later.
Those are different populations, not contradictory clocks.

**Census:** Two May 24 browser reports reached Census with malformed key-bearing URLs.[2][3]
They ended at a `Missing Key` page, not the requested data.[2][3]
The June 17 “triple-relay retrieval” is three separate wrappers: empty Jina content, AllOrigins error, and CorsProxy restriction.[4][5][6]
Two independent archive copies contain the public static file.[7]
No record links those copies to the relays or an agent.
Transluce's narrower June exposed-key statement is not contradicted.[9]

**AIHW:** The June 18 doubled-scheme Jina capture exists.[8]
Its body is a security-check page, not the diagnosis workbook.[8]
That hospital resource differs from the published pharmaceutical task.[10]
Earlier June 17–18 AIHW activity was already reported elsewhere.[13]
The exact Jina capture may be a narrower archival addition; global novelty is unproved.

**Relays:** jqp is the largest outer-host category in the inspected county-reference corpus.[19]
Its cumulative-body share is 17,086 of 46,278 occurrences, or 36.920%.[19]
The insert/replace-hunk share is 12,904 of 32,310, or 39.938%.[19]
These are textual counts, not network traffic.
The source inventory already names Lemino and the other services.[20]
The claimed 480 cors.lol references misassigns a generic CORS-proxy category.[20]
“No infrastructure was built” exceeds the observation boundary.

**Dormancy:** A scoped urlquery county query passes its June control and has no recent matches.[14][15]
Other controls or platform access failed.
GreyNoise returned an authorization failure, not a zero count.[16]
The evidence cannot establish that a stable operation stopped.

Keep the four June clusters related-looking but independent.
The useful contribution is corrected chronology, resource scope, response interpretation, and reproducible reference counts.
It is not a newly proven unified agent operation.

My earlier endorsement of “same operation” and the crawler-exclusion argument was too strong.
Those statements are withdrawn in this review.

An independent skeptical reader would likely identify a busy SEC topic and separate unsuccessful data-retrieval traces.
They would need our narrative to infer shared actors, causal handoffs, or cessation.
