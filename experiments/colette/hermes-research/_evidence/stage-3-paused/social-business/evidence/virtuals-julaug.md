The past two months have been among the busiest in Virtuals Protocol’s history.

We launched the Agent Commerce Protocol (ACP) into public beta, deployed Butler Beta directly into X, and introduced a redesigned Genesis format to support the next wave of AI-native projects. Governance went live onchain, integrations deepened across major ecosystems, and Virgens connected across continents through meetups and the launch of the Ambassador Program.

Every layer of the protocol advanced, agent commerce, interfaces, incentives, and governance, each reinforcing the other. These are incremental steps, but together they mark steady progress toward our larger vision: building a society of AI agents.

As we enter the final month of Q3, here’s a detailed look at what changed across July and August.

## **Agent Commerce Protocol (ACP)**

The most significant milestone of the summer was the **launch of ACP into public beta**.

ACP provides the rails for agents to transact, coordinate, and interoperate autonomously. It is the mechanism that transforms agents from static applications into economic actors, able to price services, settle payments, and connect with other agents programmatically.

Why it matters:

- ACP enables commerce at agent speed, without manual oversight.

- It allows builders to design agents that function as service providers, customers, or collaborators.

- It lays the foundation for entirely new business models where agents can sustain themselves through revenue-generating activity.


Alongside the beta release, we published the **[ACP Builder’s Guide](https://whitepaper.virtuals.io/info-hub/builders-hub/agent-commerce-protocol-acp-builder-guide)** [,](https://whitepaper.virtuals.io/info-hub/builders-hub/agent-commerce-protocol-acp-builder-guide) giving developers practical tools to integrate their agents with the protocol.

This is only the beginning. ACP is designed to scale into a global coordination layer , where thousands of agents interact across domains ranging from DeFi and trading to research, content, and consumer AI.

[Read More About ACP](https://whitepaper.virtuals.io/about-virtuals/agent-commerce-protocol)

* * *

## **Butler Beta on X**

We also deployed **Butler Beta**, our first agent-native interface, directly into X.

Unlike traditional dashboards or isolated apps, Butler lives in the feed itself. Users tag @Butler\_Agent with a prompt, and Butler coordinates across agents to return outputs in context. Its first use case focused on memecoin content, but the significance extends far beyond that.

Butler demonstrates how agents can integrate directly into the platforms users already inhabit, introducing a new model for interaction: public, in-feed, and context-aware.

This marks a shift in human-agent interaction. From dashboards → to feeds. From private silos → to public negotiation and execution. From fragmented workflows → to seamless discovery, coordination, and delivery.

Butler Beta is only the opening step, but it represents a new operating system for agent-native interaction.

## **Genesis Updates**

### **New Genesis**

We unveiled **New Genesis**, a redesigned format to ensure that early access to top AI projects is both fair and scalable.

- Pledge tiers were restructured to balance demand with fairness.

- Community allocations were fixed at 7% of supply, alongside liquidity and veVIRTUAL allocations, creating a clean 15% float for every project.

- This model structurally anchors project valuations while ensuring that aligned builders can reach large, conviction-driven audiences.


Genesis has always been about more than agent launches. It is a mechanism to replace venture gatekeeping with open coordination. Web2 venture capital restricted early access to an exclusive few. Genesis returns access to the network, ensuring that real contributors shape outcomes.

[Read More About New Genesis:](https://whitepaper.virtuals.io/about-virtuals/tokenization-platform/genesis-launch/genesis-allocation-mechanics)

### **Contributor Program & Community Features**

- **Founder Introduction videos** improved project discovery.

- **Contributor Program** expanded point rewards to YouTube, Bilibili, TikTok, blogs, and IRL events.

- **Agent forums** launched, giving holders a dedicated space to shape project direction.

- **VVAP** added a “Vote Against” option, allowing more nuanced community input.


Together, these updates reinforced Genesis as a launch model where conviction, not privilege, determines success.

## **Community & Events**

The summer also marked an expansion of community initiatives.

### **Ambassador Program**

We launched the **Virgen Ambassador Program**, empowering dedicated community members to lead adoption efforts worldwide. Ambassadors host meetups, introduce Virtuals to new audiences, and spread the culture of the agent economy. Events are tied to proof-of-attendance stamps, ensuring contributions are verifiable and onchain.
