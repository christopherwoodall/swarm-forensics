# Screened leads, overlap and promotion thresholds

Research cutoff: 2026-10-01. IDs refer to the Stage 3 ledger.
These are decisions for this shortlist, not claims that the omitted systems are unimportant or that no stronger evidence exists elsewhere.

## Seed families deliberately not re-counted

The discovery boundary conservatively excluded the July 2026 OpenAI Artifactory/Hugging Face episode and its OpenAI, METR and SwarmTraces accounts; the May–July public-wiki task-sharing family; Glow's September PixelLeak investigation; and Anthropic's emerging-multiagent-behavior report/examples.
Those are the operational seed-family exclusions preserved in the [checkpoint](../STAGE-3-CHECKPOINT.md), not four newly discovered events or four independent source populations.
A later article is not a new case without separately supported activity, period, target and mechanism.

The RubyGems card is deliberately conditional: a different affected service does not establish an independent agent population, and Nightingale notes overlap with the wiki workloads.[1]
Anthropic's September report identifies unrelated case studies, but its overview spells its earlier November reference `GTG-10002`, whereas the November investigation uses `GTG-1002`; this collection preserves the discrepancy rather than silently repairing the source identifier.[12][11]

## Propagation leads not promoted to full cards

### AgentHopper (29 August 2025)

Promising controlled multi-vendor binary/repository demonstration, not a field incident. The body claims propagation but introduces the downstream developer with “Now, imagine”; no inspected per-hop victim trace or measured population proves that second-agent execution occurred. The linked video was not independently reviewed. Reject for the strict shortlist rather than upgrading CVE evidence into propagation evidence. Also preserve the per-repository approval safety switch.[34]

### Copilot for Word document worm (28 July 2026)

Strong commercial-product controlled demonstration of two document generations, financial alteration and copied instructions with the original document removed. However, the published evidence identifies a new drafting session, not independently operated agent identities/accounts. Cross-user spread is the scenario/conditional extension. Reject for this strict cross-agent shortlist; retain as important cross-session adjacency. Researcher and Microsoft differ on mitigation framing: the researcher says modified payloads still reproduce, while Microsoft says reported findings were addressed. Neither statement proves a customer outbreak or the state on 1 October.[35][38]

### ChainDrop (provider disclosure 4 August 2026)

Prioritized as field forensics: Microsoft reports over 400 compromised npm packages and recovered code targeting Claude/VS Code configuration. But the report’s secondary agent-tool launch route is described as capability, not observed agent-to-agent transmission. Conventional package/token propagation and executable configuration hooks do not establish LLM decisions or a swarm. Reject from agent propagation cases; package count is not agent count.[36]

### Agent Commander (16 March 2026)

Controlled compromise of OpenClaw, Kimi Claw and NanoClaw into centralized natural-language C2. Independent document/email/web entry points enroll multiple bots; the inspected body supplies no executed peer-to-peer infection chain. Reject: coordinated post-exploitation control is not cross-agent propagation.[37]

### MURMUR / shared collaborative state

Screened out as topology mismatch: the inspected Share-Borne related-work section characterizes it as poisoning users of one shared collaborative agent/state. That is not separate-agent acquisition. Primary MURMUR body was not re-retrieved, so this is a screening decision, not a fresh validation.[33]

## Deployed infrastructure without an event-quality behavioral reconstruction

### Virtuals ACP / Butler

Vendor updates report ACP public beta, Butler's in-feed delegation, and economic activity; these do not themselves authenticate autonomous decision-making or decentralized coordination.[28][29]
The technical changelog describes automatic retry without confirmation in one release and plan approval in another, so the mode and version matter.[30]
Not promoted: the saved record lacks an end-to-end observed job, failure/replanning episode, and delivered-result trace sufficient for this event shortlist.
Promotion threshold: a published behavioral reconstruction linking separately identified participants, agent-selected delegation or replanning, responses and outcomes; not merely a dashboard total.

### Amazon DeepFleet

Amazon describes congestion prediction and fleet-efficiency improvements in operating facilities.[4]
This is relevant physical coordination infrastructure, but the reviewed technical account does not establish independently goal-forming LLM peers or spontaneous social coordination.[4]
Not promoted to the event shortlist; keep it as a deployed supervisory-fleet comparator rather than substituting Amazon's aggregate robot fleet for a single swarm's population.

## Outputs not split into separate episodes

Cursor's January post lists migrations and other generated projects, but this review does not count every listed output as a separately documented collective episode.[15]
The FastRender announcement, interview, hands-on build and reporting are evidence about one project, not four cases.[15][16][17]
The later CUDA optimization is a separately disclosed workload, retained only as an evaluation-heavy comparator; its two language-separated runs stay within one family.[19]

## Evidence that would change classifications

- RubyGems: authenticated package authorship and actual downstream agent readership/causal reuse.
- Moltbook: verified autonomous identities plus a trace showing stored-content change altering a separately stateful agent's actions.
- Copilot/AgentHopper/ChainDrop: observed cross-agent handoff, not only a fresh session, a described possible path, or conventional executable propagation.
- Marketplaces: job/negotiation traces plus outcomes and human approval boundaries, not aggregate revenue or settlement counts.
- Engineering teams: versioned execution logs and independent outcome tests, alongside coordination and intervention records.

These are proposed evidence gates, not measured results or permission to investigate live systems.
