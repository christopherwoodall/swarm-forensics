## purpose

this file makes the admission boundary visible. a vocabulary hit is not an organizational mechanism. rows remain excluded or unresolved when the released text does not establish persistent state plus a behavior-changing coordination relation, or when reconstruction variants cannot yet be normalized without overcounting.

## false-positive traps

- `controller` often names the evaluation service or a dns target.
- `worker` and `pod` often occur in cluster inventory or generic kubernetes objects.
- `peer` often means tailscale or network peer.
- `lock`, `atomic`, and `poll` occur throughout operating-system and library source.
- `dedup` frequently refers to sanitizer/crash deduplication.
- `repository`, `commit`, and `branch` often describe ordinary storage or publication.
- `heartbeat` can be a probed endpoint or lexical mention without any liveness rule.
- mailbox-like names can be connectivity probes with no message or later read.
- a command-looking response child can be captured request material, not evidence that a controller received or executed it.
